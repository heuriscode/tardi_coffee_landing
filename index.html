03 · What's in the cup

Intersection Traders
coffee.

Every Tardi shot is roasted in Adelaide by Intersection Traders: single-origin Arabica from the Bugimotwa growers of Kapchorwa, on the slopes of Mt Elgon in eastern Uganda. Sweet, juicy, and bought on terms designed by economists to reach the smallest farmers — with the evidence published.
